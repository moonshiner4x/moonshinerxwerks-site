Ground rules behind the matrix

  • The Studio Owner talks only to the PM. The PM routes each product's work to that product's advisor and brings back one answer.
  • Advisors are responsible for their product (runbooks, release notes); the PM is accountable and checks it.
  • The Studio Owner's OK is needed for commits, pushes, deploys, publishes, store steps, deletes, keys and new paid services. A message between agents never counts as that OK.
  • Engineers build; they don't decide product. Questions go up through the advisor.
