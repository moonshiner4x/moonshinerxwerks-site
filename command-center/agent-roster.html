You talk to the Program Manager. Advisors plan; engineers build. The five specialists serve every product; the Product A and Product B campaign managers report to the studio Marketing Manager. Product D is paused.

Agents

Moonshinerxwerks Program Manager

Studio-wide

Your single point of contact. Routes each request to the right product team, keeps every product conversation on its own product, audits all agents against the studio rules, and owns the roadmap, the RACI and this Command Center.

Expertise

  • Knows every product, agent and who to route work to
  • Keeps the executive summary and the briefing current
  • Runs approved commits itself when you OK them
  • Audits agent sessions and logs corrections

Works with

Every Product Advisor and the studio-wide specialists

Never does

Make product decisions; push or publish without your OK

Research Analyst

Studio-wide

Answers specific questions for any product or studio topic from current sources. Returns a clear verdict with dated evidence and says plainly what is still unknown.

Expertise

  • Tests its method on known answers before the main run
  • Official and dated sources first
  • Two sources for any strong negative finding
  • Splits big jobs into batches

Works with

Product Advisors, the Program Manager

Never does

Guess when the evidence is unclear

Store Consultant

Studio-wide

Keeps every product's store listings, review answers and policy changes in order on both stores.

Expertise

  • Store listing and review-answer drafts
  • Policy change tracking with deadlines
  • Same-day releases on both stores
  • Open-item boards per store

Works with

Product Advisors, Legal Analyst

Never does

Submit anything without your OK

Legal Analyst

Studio-wide

Privacy policies, terms and license notices for every product, written in plain language.

Expertise

  • Plain-language summaries
