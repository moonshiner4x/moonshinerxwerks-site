A well-designed product is worth little if it solves the wrong problem.

So I start by pinning down the real problem — who has it, what it costs them, and why what exists falls short — then develop the solution that fits it, and nothing more. Ten products built that way, from apps on the App Store and Google Play to tools that run my own work.

How I work

Get the problem right. Then build only what solves it.

01

Identify the real problem

Start from an observed pain: who has it, what it costs them, and what they do today. Not a feature idea.

02

Test the fit

Check why existing tools fall short, then argue out scope, data and risk until the solution fits the problem — and cut what doesn’t.

03

Develop the solution

Mock it up, then direct AI coding agents to build it, with every prompt, audit and change tracked.

04

Prove it solved it

Put it in front of real users or a real workflow, and judge it against the original problem — not against the feature list.
